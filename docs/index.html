Modeling notes

Contextual entities use UUID URNs. Reusable identifiers and designators use deterministic dir: IRIs where normalized lexical equality is the intended join rule. Information content literals use rdf:value, and generated individuals receive rdfs:label.

Names are proper names unless legal status is established. A person is normatively responsible for a telephone line, and that line is designated by the telephone number. Email uses a separate mailbox. A person resides in a geospatial region, and the postal address designates that site. Street, locality, region, postal code, country, and other populated address components are separately identified information entities linked by has continuant part. Imports preserve only raw spreadsheet fields that are not already modeled as graph structure.

The small entry extension adds classes and the resides in property. See the church ontology and bundled CCO/BFO import.
